About this viewer

Many cheap USB testers have Bluetooth, but their phone apps are slow to pair, full of ads or no longer updated. This page reads the same Bluetooth stream directly and puts every reading on one dashboard: voltage, current, power, temperature and the USB data lines each get a graph, all on the same time axis. Move the pointer over any graph to read every value at that moment, and download the whole recording as CSV. There is nothing to install and no account.

How to use it

  1. Plug the meter between a charger and the device you want to measure, and switch it on.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and choose Connect to meter.
  3. Pick the meter in the list. A J7-C shows up as UC96_BLE, ATorch meters under their model name, such as UD18-BLE or AT24-BLE. Readings start right away.

Browser support

The page uses Web Bluetooth, which Chrome and Edge provide on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS and Android. Firefox and Safari (including every browser on iPhone and iPad, except apps such as Bluefy that add Web Bluetooth) do not support it.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser. The readings are kept in memory while the page is open and are never sent anywhere. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Questions

The meter does not appear in the list.
The list shows a meter only while the browser can hear it: the meter needs power, its Bluetooth switched on, and no other connection. Disconnect it from any phone app or other tab first, because these meters accept one Bluetooth connection at a time. On Windows and Linux, make sure Bluetooth is on. The list also leaves out devices that do not look like a supported meter. If yours is ready and still missing, cancel the list and choose Show all Bluetooth devices. When the page does not know the name of a meter connected that way, it says so, and an issue with that name gets it added.
Why does it say the checksum is not verified?
Some meters, the J7-C among them, use a different checksum from the ATorch one. The readings are still shown when the frame is otherwise well formed.
Can it reset the counters or change settings?
Not yet. The viewer only reads.
