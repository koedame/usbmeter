About this viewer

Many cheap USB testers have Bluetooth, but their phone apps are slow to pair, full of ads or no longer updated. This page reads the same Bluetooth stream directly and shows it on a big screen, with a graph and a CSV download. There is nothing to install and no account.

How to use it

  1. Plug the meter between a charger and the device you want to measure, and switch it on.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and choose Connect to meter.
  3. Pick the meter in the list (names look like UD18-BLE or AT24-BLE). Readings start right away.

If your meter shows up in the Bluetooth list but no readings appear, please open an issue with its name and model.

Browser support

The page uses Web Bluetooth, which Chrome and Edge provide on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS and Android. Firefox and Safari (including every browser on iPhone and iPad, except apps such as Bluefy that add Web Bluetooth) do not support it.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser. The readings are kept in memory while the page is open and are never sent anywhere. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Questions

The meter does not appear in the list.
Disconnect it from any phone app first. These meters accept one Bluetooth connection at a time. On Windows and Linux, make sure Bluetooth is on.
Why does it say the checksum is not verified?
Some meters, the J7-C among them, use a different checksum from the ATorch one. The readings are still shown when the frame is otherwise well formed.
Can it reset the counters or change settings?
Not yet. The viewer only reads.
